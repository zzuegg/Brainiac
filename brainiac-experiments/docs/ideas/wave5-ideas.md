# Brainiac: wave 5 ideas

*Compiled 2026-11-17 against `claude/project-thread-8zvcl0`, plus the WP6 branch `worktree-agent-ab4dbd9fdc0641a2d`. Waves 1–4 implemented about 400 techniques, and all 85 items of `unimplemented-ideas.md` are done. This list comes from a 2024–2026 literature sweep:*

- *NeurIPS 2025 best papers, ICLR 2025/2026 outstanding papers, ICML 2025/2026 outstanding papers*
- *DeepSeek-V3.2/V4, Kimi K2/Linear, Qwen3-Next/3.5, Gemma 3n and OLMo 3 reports*
- *vLLM/SGLang features, optimizer papers, interpretability work and robot-policy papers*
- *papers on evaluation, data, privacy and robustness*

*Every item was grepped (case-insensitive, by class names, paper names and javadoc keywords) over `*/src/main` in the main tree and in all `.claude/worktrees/*`. An item is listed only when nothing matched, or when the only matches were unrelated. One example of an unrelated match is the regex "Quest" matching the word "request".*

## How this was checked

- **Already implemented, so left out.** These techniques turned up in the sweep but already have a class:
  - Gated attention (NeurIPS 2025 best paper): `GatedAttention`.
  - Energy-Based Transformers: `EnergyRefiner`.
  - TopK SAE: `SparseAutoencoder`.
  - Tuned lens: `LogitLens`.
  - Wanda, SparseGPT and 2:4 sparsity: `WeightPruning`.
  - ShortGPT layer removal: `BlockImportance`.
  - LayerSkip self-speculation: `EarlyExit`.
  - Confidence-ordered unmasking for masked diffusion (ICML 2025 outstanding, Kim et al.): `MaskedDiffusion`'s low-confidence remasking.
  - MXFP4/NVFP4 as a storage format: `MicroscalingFormat`.
  - ReDo-style dormant-unit resets: `ContinualBackprop`.
  - ROME: `RomeEditor`.
  - MeZO: `MeZo`.
  - Clip-higher: `GrpoTrainer`.
  - From the open-model reports: mHC, Engram, nested learning (`ContinuumMemoryBlock`), MLA, NSA, MuonClip, Scion, SSMax, iRoPE, Gated DeltaNet and the 3:1 hybrid stack.
  - Recurrent memory, Titans, TTT, HRM and TRM.
  - DiLoCo and FedAvg (`DiLoCo` and `FederatedAveraging` in `experimental.train`).
- **Considered but cut** (to stay within 6 × 18, or too close to an existing block):
  - Duo uniform-state diffusion.
  - Ridge-regression grokking (ICML 2026 HM), which is close to `GrokkingExperiment`.
  - The superposition-scaling toy (NeurIPS 2025 runner-up), which is close to `SuperpositionRecovery`.
  - Text-to-LoRA and p-diff weight generation.
  - Benchmark signal-to-noise, folded into 2.17.
  - P-EAGLE, folded into 4.1.
  - LongRoPE2.
  - TextGrad/GEPA prompt optimisation, which needs a stronger LM than the toy ones.
- **CPU-toy filter.** Every item names a small task that already exists in `brainiac-experiments` (or a tiny new one) and a baseline already in the repo. Items that only show a benefit at scale were dropped. This removed most kernel or multi-GPU work, such as DeepEP, FP8 GEMM tuning and FSDP.

**Where an item should live:**

- `experimental.<pkg>`: a new block in `brainiac-experiments`.
- `experiments.<theme>`: a new or extended runnable experiment.
- **core-worthy**: belongs in a core module once measured.
